VaultPony 2.0.0. An unlocked vault is now writable from the Files app and other apps. Auto-lock can wait up to 1 hour, or until you lock it. Unlock has a Hash menu (#5, #6). New: bulk import and export with folders (#3), video and audio playback, share into a vault, grid with thumbnails, sort, search, multi-select, camera capture, TrueCrypt mode with Convert to VeraCrypt, read-only ext4. Camera and microphone are asked for only on first use.
