VaultPony 2.1.0. Fixed: in large FAT vaults, the top folder could show junk files and folders once it held many files. This version removes them when the vault is unlocked, and tells you if any real files may have been damaged. Importing many files is much faster, and AES now uses the phone's hardware. The camera zooms by pinch or by tapping a zoom step, and focuses where you tap. Settings is now a short page, with separate pages for the vault list, auto-lock, language and About.
